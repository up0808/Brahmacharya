# Brahmacharya

Neeche Shri Hit Premanand Ji Maharaj (jinhe "Premanand Ji Maharaj / Govind Sharan" bhi likha jata hai) se prerit ek updated plan + techniques di gayi hain, jo aapko zyada rooted, effective aur spiritually aligned tarike se Brahmacharya ka palan karne mein madad karengi.

Main inhe aapke liye ek revised daily-lifetime routine mein include karunga.

## 📜 Shri Hit Premanand Ji Maharaj se Techniques aur Principles

Maine unki teachings (Bhajan Marg, unki books, pravachan) se jo mil saka, collect kiya hai. Vivek ka use karein: ye guiding ideas hain, inhe carefully adopt karein.

Yahan Brahmacharya maintain karne ke liye Premanand Ji ki key techniques / rules / cautions hain:

### Technique / Principle aur Explanation

**1. Aath Activities jo Brahmacharya ko Destroy karti hain**

Premanand Ji 8 "sexual indulgences" ka naam lete hain jo Brahmacharya ke fall ki taraf le jaati hain. Inmein shamil hain:

1. Sensual thoughts, visuals ya imagination (erotic content padhna ya dekhna)
2. Obscene talk, discussions
3. Gestures, joking, privacy mein physical touch
4. Lust ke saath secret gazing
5. Desire ke saath flirtatious talk, smiling
6. Attraction ke liye body ko adorn karna
7. Sensual pleasure ki talks sunna
8. Physical intercourse / masturbation

In sabhi se bachen. Ise ek checklist ke roop mein use karein: jab bhi koi thought ya action inmein se kisi ke paas aaye, ruko, reflect karo, attention divert karo.

**2. Gaze aur Company ka Restraint**

- Doosron ko lustfully na dekhein (secret ya overt).
- Aisi company na rakhein jo impure thoughts stimulate kare.
- Opposite sex ke saath privacy mein akele na baithen.
- Agar company sensual topics ki taraf shift ho toh khud ko door karein.

Agar aap apni gaze wandering notice karein, toh gently kisi spiritual thought par refocus karein. Friends / environment ko wisely choose karein.

**3. Attraction ke liye Adornment, Fashion, Makeup se Bachen**

- Doosron ko attract karne ke liye body ko decorate na karein: perfumes, touch-up, stylish clothes, etc.
- Koi bhi adornment devotional nature ki honi chahiye (jaise, Guru ke feet ki dust, temple se garland) na ki vanity ke liye.

Ye subtle hai: kabhi-kabhi attraction ke liye dressing ke small acts desire ko feed kar sakte hain. Dress mein humble rahein.

**4. Diet aur Digestion**

- Sattvic food khayein (pure, simple, non-stimulating)
- Heavy, spicy, bahut oily, sour, onion, garlic, etc. se bachen.
- Tea, coffee se bachen (specially younger aspirants ke liye)
- Overeating ya raat mein late khane se bachen.
- Food ko slowly aur thoroughly chew karein.
- Bowels regular rakhein: constipation se bachen (kyunki impure energy body ko agitate kar sakti hai).

Agar digestion poor hai, toh body restless ho jata hai, jo desires provoke kar sakta hai. Toh digestive health purity support karti hai.

**5. Bathing, Cleanliness aur Ventilation**

- Agar possible ho toh freshness aur purity maintain karne ke liye din mein 2-3 baar bath karein.
- Surroundings ko well-ventilated, fresh air rakhein.
- Personal hygiene strict rakhein.

Kabhi-kabhi small neglects (odor, dust) mind ke liye irritants ban sakti hain. Ek fresh body ek calm mind ko support karta hai.

**6. Morning Routine aur Late Wake / Sleep se Bachna**

- Jaldi uthein, ideally Brahma-muhurta mein (around 4-6 AM).
- Waking ke baad, Vajrasana mein baith kar warm water piyein, restroom se pehle 300-400 steps chalein.
- Pranayama / gentle stretching karein.
- Late sone ya oversleeping se bachen.
- Heavy meal ya late night ke baad immediately sone se bachen.

Morning mind control ke liye powerful time hai. Agar aap late start karte hain, toh mind disorder ka aadi ho jata hai.

**7. Reflection aur Self-Examination**

- Din ke end mein, apne din ki review karein: kab impure thoughts aaye, kitni der, kya unhe trigger kiya.
- Notes banayein: "Maine ye dekha, wo socha, is tarah react kiya"
- Decide karein ki kal un triggers se kaise avoid karna hai.

Ye awareness build karta hai. Time ke saath, aap pitfalls ka anticipate karenge before wo happen.

**8. Chanting / Name Recitation / Devotional Practices**

- Desires ko God ka naam chanting (Radha-Naam, ya aapka mantra), devotional songs, scripture reading se replace karein.
- Mind ko higher thoughts mein engaged rakhein.
- God ke baare mein discourses sunein ya discuss karein, sensual topics par nahi.

Mind ko ek focus ki zaroorat hai. Agar aap ise idle chhod dete hain, toh desires sneak in ho jaati hain.

**9. Inner Renunciation / Detachment**

- Bodily comforts, sensory pleasures se detachment cultivate karein.
- Hardship, simple clothes accept karein, heat/cold endure karein.
- Comfort ko refuge ke roop mein na pakden.
- Vanity ya ego mein indulge na karein.

Time ke saath, aap external comfort par kam dependent aur zyada self-reliant ho jate hain.

---

**Important Caveat:** Inmein se kuch rules zyada strict ya austere hain than what ek student modern life mein practical paa sakta hai. Wisdom use karein. Aap gradually adopt kar sakte hain — ek baar mein sab kuch nahi.

## 🔄 Revised Daily Lifetime Routine (Premanand Ji ki Techniques ko Incorporate karte hue)

Yahan aapki earlier plan + in deeper techniques ko combine karte hue ek structured lifetime daily chart hai. Aap ise apne everyday guide ke roop mein use kar sakte hain.

### 🌄 Morning (4:00 AM – lagbhag 8:00 AM)

| Time | Activity | Purpose / Notes |
|------|---------|-----------------|
| 4:00 AM (ya earlier) | Brahma-muhurta mein jagein | Quiet pre-dawn time purity aur mental power support karta hai. |
| 4:00–4:10 | Vajrasana mein ~300–500 ml warm water piyein | Digestive fire wake karne aur body purify karne mein help karta hai. |
| 4:10–4:20 | ~300–400 steps chalein | Constipation se relief aur prana balance karne mein help karta hai. |
| 4:20–4:40 | Abhyasa: pranayama / gentle stretching / asana | Energy ko activate aur transform karein. |
| 4:40–5:00 | Bath aur freshen up | Body ko clean, pure rakhein; over-adorning se bachen. |
| 5:00–5:30 | Meditation / Chanting / Mantra / Naam Japa | Mind ko divine focus par stabilize karein. |
| 5:30–6:30 | Morning study / spiritual reading / scriptures | Calm mind ke saath kaam karein before external demands begin hon. |

### ☀️ Daytime (6:30 AM – Evening)

| Period | Activity | Precautions aur Tips |
|--------|---------|----------------------|
| 6:30–8:00 | Breakfast aur light refresh | Kuch sattvic khayein; heavy spicy ya junk food se bachen. |

| 5:30–6:00 | Evening snack (agar zaroorat ho) | Light, sattvic only — jaise fruits, nuts, light liquids. |

| Time | Activity | Purpose / Precautions |

## 📅 Weekly / Monthly Practices aur Reminders

- **Weekly reflection** (Sunday ya ek fixed day): Successes aur slips ki review karein; persistent triggers identify karein; unse avoid karne ki plan banayein.

- **Satsang / Discourses / Devotional Gatherings:** Spiritually uplifting gatherings attend karein; God / devotion par discourses sunein; sensual content talks se bachen.

- **Service / Seva:** Aise tasks mein engage hon jo doosron ya Guru / temple community ki serve karte hain — apni energy outward channel karein.

- **Possessions / dressing simplify karein:** Week mein ek baar ya monthly, assess karein ki koi belonging ya clothing desire stimulate kar rahi hai. Simplify karein.

- **Periodic fasting / observance (optional):** Certain spiritual days par, body aur mind purify karne ke liye light fast ya special observance karein (health caution ke saath).

## ✅ Things to Watch / Avoid (Premanand Ji ke Lens se)

- Erotic / romantic content padhna
- Sensual movies, images dekhna
- Obscene talk, jokes, flirtatious gestures
- Kisi ko sexually touch karna ya opposite sex ke saath privacy mein baithna
- Desire ke saath doosron ki beauty admire karna
- Nighttime indulgence (masturbation, late-night fantasies)
- Dark, confined, stuffy rooms mein rehna
- Unclean body, unkempt environment
- Bahut heavy, stimulant foods khana
- Bahut late sona ya oversleeping
- Bad company, peer pressure

8 destructive activities ko ek clear "don't cross this line" list ke roop mein use karein.

## 🧠 Apni Will ke saath Integration (Kaise Begin aur Sustain karein)

**Gradually start karein** — Immediately sab kuch perfect karne ki koshish na karein; pehle 2-3 items (diet, morning routine, gaze control) pick karein.

**Reminders / alarms use karein** — Wake-up, meditation, breaks, chanting ke liye.

**Ek symbolic object pehenein** — jaise ek small thread, bead, tilak, jo aapko apni purity ki vow yaad dilaye.

**Ek higher purpose se anchor karein** — Yaad rakhein: ye sirf abstaining nahi hai, ye spiritual growth, knowledge, service ki taraf energy redirect karna hai.

**Mistakes hone par self-compassion** — Slip ke baad give up na karein. Ise note karein, analyze karein, aur recommit karein.

**Ek Guru / community / support group seek karein** — Koi jo samajhta hai aur aapko guide kar sakta hai.

**Daily read aur reflect karein** — Conviction strengthen karne ke liye Premanand Ji ke works ya similar teaching padhna continue karein.
