📜 Techniques & Principles from Shri Hit Premanand Ji Maharaj

I collected what I could find from his teachings (Bhajan Marg, his books, discourses). Use discretion: these are guiding ideas, adapt them carefully.

Here are key techniques / rules / cautions from Premanand Ji for maintaining Brahmacharya:

Technique / Principle	Explanation & Practical Steps	Notes / Source

Eight Activities That Destroy Brahmacharya	Premanand Ji names 8 “sexual indulgences” that lead to the fall of brahmacharya.  <br>These include: <br>1. Sensual thoughts, visuals or imagination (reading or seeing erotic content) <br>2. Obscene talk, discussions <br>3. Gestures, joking, physical touch in privacy <br>4. Secret gazing with lust <br>5. Flirtatious talk, smiling with desire <br>6. Adorning the body for attraction <br>7. Listening to talks of sensual pleasure <br>8. Physical intercourse / masturbation <br>— Avoid all of these.	Use this as a checklist: whenever a thought or action approaches any of these, stop, reflect, divert attention.
Restraint of Gaze & Company	Do not look lustfully at others (secret or overt). <br>Don’t keep company that stimulates impure thoughts. <br>Don’t sit alone in private with opposite sex. <br>Distance yourself if the company shifts to sensual topics. 	If you notice your gaze wandering, gently refocus on a spiritual thought. Also choose friends / environment wisely.
Avoid Adornment, Fashion, Makeup for Attraction	Do not decorate body to attract others: perfumes, touch-up, stylish clothes, etc. <br>Any adornment should be devotional in nature (e.g., dust of Guru’s feet, garland from temple) not for vanity. 	This is subtle: sometimes small acts of dressing for attraction can feed desire. Be humble in dress.
Diet & Digestion	Eat sattvic food (pure, simple, non-stimulating) <br>Avoid heavy, spicy, too oily, sour, onion, garlic, etc. <br>Avoid tea, coffee (especially for younger aspirants) <br>Avoid overeating or eating late at night. <br>Chew food slowly and thoroughly. <br>Keep bowels regular: avoid constipation (since impure energy can agitate body). 	If digestion is poor, the body becomes restless, which can provoke desires. So digestive health supports purity.
Bathing, Cleanliness & Ventilation	Bath 2–3 times a day if possible to maintain freshness and purity. <br>Keep surroundings well-ventilated, fresh air. <br>Keep personal hygiene strict. 	Sometimes small neglects (odor, dust) can become irritants to the mind. A fresh body supports a calm mind.
Morning Routine & Avoiding Late Wake / Sleep	Rise early, ideally in the Brahma-muhurta (around 4–6 AM). <br>After waking, drink warm water while seated in Vajrasana, walk 300–400 steps before the restroom. <br>Do pranayama / gentle stretching. <br>Avoid sleeping in late or oversleeping. <br>Avoid sleeping immediately after a heavy meal or late night. 	Morning is powerful time for mind control. If you start late, the mind gets used to disorder.
Reflection & Self-Examination	At day's end, review your day: when impure thoughts came, how long, what triggered them. <br>Make notes: “I saw this, thought that, reacted this way” <br>Decide how to avoid those triggers tomorrow. 	This builds awareness. Over time, you’ll anticipate pitfalls before they happen.
Chanting / Name Recitation / Devotional Practices	Replace desires with chanting God’s name (Radha-Naam, or your mantra), devotional songs, reading scripture. <br>Keep the mind engaged with higher thoughts. <br>Discuss or hear discourses about God, not on sensual topics. 	The mind needs a focus. If you leave it idle, desires sneak in.
Inner Renunciation / Detachment	Cultivate detachment from bodily comforts, sensory pleasures. <br>Accept hardship, simple clothes, endure heat/cold. <br>Don’t cling to comfort as a refuge. <br>Don’t indulge in vanity or ego. 	Over time, you become less dependent on external comfort and more self-reliant.


> Important caveat: Some of these rules are stricter or more austere than what a student may find practical in modern life. Use wisdom. You may adopt gradually — not all at once.

🔄 Revised Daily Lifetime Routine (Incorporating Premanand Ji’s Techniques)

Here’s a structured lifetime daily chart combining your earlier plan + these deeper techniques. You can use this as your everyday guide.


---

🌄 Morning (4:00 AM – about 8:00 AM)

Time	Activity	Purpose / Notes

4:00 AM (or earlier)	Wake up in Brahma-muhurta	The quiet pre-dawn time supports purity & mental power.
4:00–4:10	Drink ~300–500 ml warm water in Vajrasana	Helps wake digestive fire & purify the body.
4:10–4:20	Walk ~300–400 steps	Helps relieve constipation and balance prana.
4:20–4:40	Abhyasa: pranayama / gentle stretching / asana	Activate and transform energy.
4:40–5:00	Bath & freshen	Keep body clean, pure; avoid over-adorning.
5:00–5:30	Meditation / Chanting / Mantra / Naam Japa	Stabilize the mind on divine focus.
5:30–6:30	Morning study / spiritual reading / scriptures	Work with a calm mind before external demands begin.



---

☀️ Daytime (6:30 AM – Evening)

Period	Activity	Precautions & Tips

6:30–8:00	Breakfast & light refresh	Eat something sattvic; avoid heavy spicy or junk food.
8:00–12:00	Main academic / work session	Focus without distractions; avoid lustful thoughts, obscene content.
12:00–12:30	Lunch (sattvic, light)	Chew thoroughly; avoid overeating or leftover / stale foods.
12:30–1:00	Rest / light walk (optional)	Not full sleep; short rest okay, but don’t nap too long.
1:00–4:00	Continue studies / tasks	Remain alert to inner state; when mind wanders, re-anchor to Naam or prayer.
4:00–5:30	Physical exercise / yoga / sports	Use physical activity to deplete excessive energy healthily.
5:30–6:00	Evening snack (if needed)	Light, sattvic only — e.g. fruits, nuts, light liquids.



---

🌆 Evening to Night (6:00 PM – Sleep)

Time	Activity	Purpose / Precautions

6:30–7:30	Self-study / review / spiritual reading	Avoid sensual or provocative literature.
7:30–8:00	Dinner (light, early)	Avoid heavy food. Finish dinner at least 1.5–2 hours before sleep.
8:00–8:30	Chanting / Bhajan / Naam Japa / Kirtan	Affirm purity before the night.
8:30–9:00	Reflective journaling	Note any impure thoughts, how you handled them, triggers for next day.
9:00–9:30	Relaxation (gentle prayer, soft reading)	Prepare the mind to settle.
9:30–10:00	Sleep (ideally before or by 10:00)	Avoid screen, sensual content before bed.

📅 Weekly / Monthly Practices & Reminders

Weekly reflection (Sunday or one fixed day):
Review the successes & slips; identify persistent triggers; plan how to avoid them.

Satsang / Discourses / Devotional Gatherings:
Attend spiritually uplifting gatherings; listen to discourses on God / devotion; avoid sensual content talks.

Service / Seva:
Engage in tasks that serve others or the Guru / temple community — channel your energy outward.

Simplify possessions / dressing:
Once a week or monthly, assess whether any belonging or clothing is stimulating desire. Simplify.

Periodic fasting / observance (optional):
On certain spiritual days, do light fast or special observance to purify body & mind (with health caution).

Admiring others’ beauty with desire

Unclean body, unkempt environment

Sleeping too late or oversleeping

Bad company, peer pressure


Use the 8 destructive activities as a clear “don’t cross this line” list. 


---

🧠 Integrating with Your Will (How to Begin & Sustain)

1. Start gradually — Don’t try to perfect everything immediately; pick 2–3 items (diet, morning routine, gaze control) first.


2. Use reminders / alarms — For wake-up, meditation, breaks, chanting.


3. Wear a symbolic object — e.g. a small thread, bead, tilak, to remind you of your vow of purity.


4. Anchor to a higher purpose — Remember: it’s not just abstaining, it’s redirecting energy toward spiritual growth, knowledge, service.


5. Self-compassion when mistakes happen — Don’t give up after a slip. Note it, analyze, and recommit.


6. Seek a Guru / community / support group — Someone who understands and can guide you.


7. Read and reflect daily — Continue reading Premanand Ji’s works or similar teaching to strengthen conviction.
