# Brahmacharya

Below is an updated plan + techniques inspired by Shri Hit Premanand Ji Maharaj (also spelled "Premanand Ji Maharaj / Govind Sharan") to help you follow Brahmacharya in a more rooted, effective, and spiritually aligned way.

I'll then fold these into a revised daily-lifetime routine for you.

## 📜 Techniques & Principles from Shri Hit Premanand Ji Maharaj

I collected what I could find from his teachings (Bhajan Marg, his books, discourses). Use discretion: these are guiding ideas, adapt them carefully.

Here are key techniques / rules / cautions from Premanand Ji for maintaining Brahmacharya:

### Technique / Principle and Explanation

**1. Eight Activities That Destroy Brahmacharya**

Premanand Ji names 8 "sexual indulgences" that lead to the fall of brahmacharya. These include:

1. Sensual thoughts, visuals or imagination (reading or seeing erotic content)
2. Obscene talk, discussions
3. Gestures, joking, physical touch in privacy
4. Secret gazing with lust
5. Flirtatious talk, smiling with desire
6. Adorning the body for attraction
7. Listening to talks of sensual pleasure
8. Physical intercourse / masturbation

Avoid all of these. Use this as a checklist: whenever a thought or action approaches any of these, stop, reflect, divert attention.

**2. Restraint of Gaze & Company**

- Do not look lustfully at others (secret or overt).
- Don't keep company that stimulates impure thoughts.
- Don't sit alone in private with opposite sex.
- Distance yourself if the company shifts to sensual topics.

If you notice your gaze wandering, gently refocus on a spiritual thought. Also choose friends / environment wisely.

**3. Avoid Adornment, Fashion, Makeup for Attraction**

- Do not decorate body to attract others: perfumes, touch-up, stylish clothes, etc.
- Any adornment should be devotional in nature (e.g., dust of Guru's feet, garland from temple) not for vanity.

This is subtle: sometimes small acts of dressing for attraction can feed desire. Be humble in dress.

**4. Diet & Digestion**

- Eat sattvic food (pure, simple, non-stimulating)
- Avoid heavy, spicy, too oily, sour, onion, garlic, etc.
- Avoid tea, coffee (especially for younger aspirants)
- Avoid overeating or eating late at night.
- Chew food slowly and thoroughly.
- Keep bowels regular: avoid constipation (since impure energy can agitate body).

If digestion is poor, the body becomes restless, which can provoke desires. So digestive health supports purity.

**5. Bathing, Cleanliness & Ventilation**

- Bath 2–3 times a day if possible to maintain freshness and purity.
- Keep surroundings well-ventilated, fresh air.
- Keep personal hygiene strict.

Sometimes small neglects (odor, dust) can become irritants to the mind. A fresh body supports a calm mind.

**6. Morning Routine & Avoiding Late Wake / Sleep**

- Rise early, ideally in the Brahma-muhurta (around 4–6 AM).
- After waking, drink warm water while seated in Vajrasana, walk 300–400 steps before the restroom.
- Do pranayama / gentle stretching.
- Avoid sleeping in late or oversleeping.
- Avoid sleeping immediately after a heavy meal or late night.

Morning is powerful time for mind control. If you start late, the mind gets used to disorder.

**7. Reflection & Self-Examination**

- At day's end, review your day: when impure thoughts came, how long, what triggered them.
- Make notes: "I saw this, thought that, reacted this way"
- Decide how to avoid those triggers tomorrow.

This builds awareness. Over time, you'll anticipate pitfalls before they happen.

**8. Chanting / Name Recitation / Devotional Practices**

- Replace desires with chanting God's name (Radha-Naam, or your mantra), devotional songs, reading scripture.
- Keep the mind engaged with higher thoughts.
- Discuss or hear discourses about God, not on sensual topics.

The mind needs a focus. If you leave it idle, desires sneak in.

**9. Inner Renunciation / Detachment**

- Cultivate detachment from bodily comforts, sensory pleasures.
- Accept hardship, simple clothes, endure heat/cold.
- Don't cling to comfort as a refuge.
- Don't indulge in vanity or ego.

Over time, you become less dependent on external comfort and more self-reliant.

---

**Important caveat:** Some of these rules are stricter or more austere than what a student may find practical in modern life. Use wisdom. You may adopt gradually — not all at once.

## 🔄 Revised Daily Lifetime Routine (Incorporating Premanand Ji's Techniques)

Here's a structured lifetime daily chart combining your earlier plan + these deeper techniques. You can use this as your everyday guide.

### 🌄 Morning (4:00 AM – about 8:00 AM)

| Time | Activity | Purpose / Notes |
|------|---------|-----------------|
| 4:00 AM (or earlier) | Wake up in Brahma-muhurta | The quiet pre-dawn time supports purity & mental power. |
| 4:00–4:10 | Drink ~300–500 ml warm water in Vajrasana | Helps wake digestive fire & purify the body. |
| 4:10–4:20 | Walk ~300–400 steps | Helps relieve constipation and balance prana. |
| 4:20–4:40 | Abhyasa: pranayama / gentle stretching / asana | Activate and transform energy. |
| 4:40–5:00 | Bath & freshen | Keep body clean, pure; avoid over-adorning. |
| 5:00–5:30 | Meditation / Chanting / Mantra / Naam Japa | Stabilize the mind on divine focus. |
| 5:30–6:30 | Morning study / spiritual reading / scriptures | Work with a calm mind before external demands begin. |

### ☀️ Daytime (6:30 AM – Evening)

| Period | Activity | Precautions & Tips |
|--------|---------|-------------------|
| 6:30–8:00 | Breakfast & light refresh | Eat something sattvic; avoid heavy spicy or junk food. |
| 8:00–12:00 | Main academic / work session | Focus without distractions; avoid lustful thoughts, obscene content. |
| 12:00–12:30 | Lunch (sattvic, light) | Chew thoroughly; avoid overeating or leftover / stale foods. |
| 12:30–1:00 | Rest / light walk (optional) | Not full sleep; short rest okay, but don't nap too long. |
| 1:00–4:00 | Continue studies / tasks | Remain alert to inner state; when mind wanders, re-anchor to Naam or prayer. |
| 4:00–5:30 | Physical exercise / yoga / sports | Use physical activity to deplete excessive energy healthily. |
| 5:30–6:00 | Evening snack (if needed) | Light, sattvic only — e.g. fruits, nuts, light liquids. |

### 🌆 Evening to Night (6:00 PM – Sleep)

| Time | Activity | Purpose / Precautions |
|------|----------|-----------------------|
| 6:30–7:30 | Self-study / review / spiritual reading | Avoid sensual or provocative literature. |
| 7:30–8:00 | Dinner (light, early) | Avoid heavy food. Finish dinner at least 1.5–2 hours before sleep. |
| 8:00–8:30 | Chanting / Bhajan / Naam Japa / Kirtan | Affirm purity before the night. |
| 8:30–9:00 | Reflective journaling | Note any impure thoughts, how you handled them, triggers for next day. |
| 8:30–9:00 | Relaxation (gentle prayer, soft reading) | Prepare the mind to settle. |
| 9:30–10:00 | Sleep (ideally before or by 10:00) | Avoid screen, sensual content before bed. |

## 📅 Weekly / Monthly Practices & Reminders

- **Weekly reflection** (Sunday or one fixed day): Review the successes & slips; identify persistent triggers; plan how to avoid them.

- **Satsang / Discourses / Devotional Gatherings:** Attend spiritually uplifting gatherings; listen to discourses on God / devotion; avoid sensual content talks.

- **Service / Seva:** Engage in tasks that serve others or the Guru / temple community — channel your energy outward.

- **Simplify possessions / dressing:** Once a week or monthly, assess whether any belonging or clothing is stimulating desire. Simplify.

- **Periodic fasting / observance (optional):** On certain spiritual days, do light fast or special observance to purify body & mind (with health caution).

## ✅ Things to Watch / Avoid (with Premanand Ji Lens)

- Reading erotic / romantic content
- Watching sensual movies, images
- Obscene talk, jokes, flirtatious gestures
- Touching someone sexually or sitting in private with opposite sex
- Admiring others' beauty with desire
- Nighttime indulgence (masturbation, late-night fantasies)
- Being in dark, confined, stuffy rooms
- Unclean body, unkempt environment
- Eating too heavy, stimulant foods
- Sleeping too late or oversleeping
- Bad company, peer pressure

Use the 8 destructive activities as a clear "don't cross this line" list.

## 🧠 Integrating with Your Will (How to Begin & Sustain)

**Start gradually** — Don't try to perfect everything immediately; pick 2–3 items (diet, morning routine, gaze control) first.

**Use reminders / alarms** — For wake-up, meditation, breaks, chanting.

**Wear a symbolic object** — e.g. a small thread, bead, tilak, to remind you of your vow of purity.

**Anchor to a higher purpose** — Remember: it's not just abstaining, it's redirecting energy toward spiritual growth, knowledge, service.

**Self-compassion when mistakes happen** — Don't give up after a slip. Note it, analyze, and recommit.

**Seek a Guru / community / support group** — Someone who understands and can guide you.

**Read and reflect daily** — Continue reading Premanand Ji's works or similar teaching to strengthen conviction.
